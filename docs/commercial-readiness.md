# Commercial readiness

## Wedge

Start with leaders who already coordinate a distributed direct-selling team through WhatsApp plus a company portal. Sell operational leverage, not “CRM features”.

## Pilot hypothesis

A leader pays when the product reliably reduces closing-day hunting, repeated message interpretation and forgotten order stages. The first pilot should measure baseline versus product for two consecutive weekly cycles.

## Metrics

- median minutes from receipt to completion;
- unresolved orders at closing;
- orders processed per active workspace/week;
- return in the next weekly cycle;
- manual corrections/rework;
- number of active workspaces.

Do not collect message contents, consultant notes, names or phones in analytics.

## Pricing experiment

Test a simple monthly price per workspace, not per consultant. Avoid annual contracts in beta. Price after observing saved time and avoided closing errors; do not anchor to spreadsheet software pricing.

## Expansion rule

A feature is eligible only when it removes repeated work, reduces closing risk, improves activation/retention, is requested by multiple paying workspaces, or is necessary for legal/security operation.

Likely later candidates: official WhatsApp Business Platform integration, assisted audio/photo parsing, configurable vocabulary for other direct-selling companies, import/export, billing and admin support. Each requires its own demand/compliance gate.

## Before first paid customer

1. Choose commercial name after trademark clearance; keep VoeTupper as repository codename only.
2. Identify the legal operating entity and controller/operator roles.
3. Finalize privacy notice, terms, retention/deletion policy and data-subject channel.
4. Wire Supabase Auth and persistence; test RLS cross-tenant isolation.
5. Configure backups, monitoring and incident process.
6. Validate the workflow with the pilot leader for at least two weekly cycles.
7. Remove any third-party brand implication from marketing and use compatibility references only when legally reviewed.

## Market basis

The product category is direct-selling operations, not a Tupperware-only tool. Brazil's direct-selling sector is large enough to support a narrow wedge and later horizontal expansion. Market claims used in sales material must be refreshed from primary industry sources before publication.
